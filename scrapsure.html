Scrapsure

A smarter way to manage shipments, documents, and operational records.

Built for recycling and scrap businesses that need better control over shipment operations, Purchase Orders, quantities, and documentation.

Explore Scrapsure with Pravaah See the product

Built around the work

From purchase order to complete shipment record.

A Purchase Order can be fulfilled through multiple shipments. Each shipment records the quantity it fulfills against that Purchase Order. Each shipment has its relevant documents attached. This creates a complete digital shipment record.

Purchase Order

Rates, last dates, quantities, and descriptions in one place.

Multiple shipments

Quantity fulfilled

Documents
