Explore Scrapsure

Initial focus

One connected operational system for recycling and scrap businesses.

These businesses often manage work through WhatsApp, Excel, paper, PDFs, photos, and scattered records across people. Scrapsure is built to bring that into one place: companies, users, Purchase Orders, shipments, quantities, and documents.

A Purchase Order can be fulfilled through multiple shipments. Each shipment records the quantity it fulfills and carries its documents — invoices, e-way bills, weighment slips, material photographs, and more — into a complete digital record.
